# Avani Loan Services - Address Update Plan

This document outlines the step-by-step process for updating your business address across digital platforms, specifically focusing on Google My Business (GMB) and Meta Business Manager.

## 1. Meta Business Manager Update
*It is highly recommended to update your Meta Business address to match your website to prevent your account from being flagged for inconsistencies.*

### Steps:
1. **Prepare Documentation:** Ensure you have a valid utility bill, bank statement, or GST certificate bearing the new Pune address: `Crescent Exclusive, flat 10 3rd floor, 1162/3, kapote lone, behind pune observatory, khaon galli near Shimla office, shivaji nagar, pune - 411005`.
2. **Login:** Go to [business.facebook.com/settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings) and select your Avani Loan Services business account.
3. **Navigate to Business Info:** Scroll down on the left sidebar and click on **Business Info**.
4. **Edit Details:** Click **Edit** next to your Business Details.
5. **Update Address:** Enter the new Pune address exactly as it appears on your legal documents.
6. **Save & Verify:** Click Save. If Meta prompts you for re-verification, follow the flow to upload the document you prepared in step 1.

## 2. Google My Business (GMB) Setup for Latur

Since you no longer have a physical office in Latur but still want to target the location, you should set up a **Service-Area Business (SAB)** profile. This allows you to appear in Latur searches without displaying a physical address.

### Steps:
1. **Create/Claim Profile:** Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/create/new) and log in with your business Gmail account.
2. **Business Name:** Enter "Avani Loan Services".
3. **Business Category:** Enter "Financial Consultant" or "Loan Agency".
4. **Location Prompt:** When asked *"Do you want to add a location customers can visit, like a store or office?"*, select **No**. (This is crucial for SABs).
5. **Service Areas:** Add "Latur, Maharashtra" and any surrounding areas you serve.
6. **Contact Info:** Add your phone number and website (`https://www.avanifinserv.com/`).
7. **Verification Address:** Google will still ask for a mailing address to send a verification postcard. Enter your home address in Latur. **Note:** This address will *not* be shown to the public.
8. **Verify:** Wait for the postcard (usually 12 days) or complete the video verification if prompted.
