  - Contact details locked: `+91 91756 35165` and `avanifinserv.com`.
  - Negative prompt constraints enforced: Zero fake bank logos, zero fake approval certificates, zero fake CIBIL score screenshots.

### B. Video Asset Engine (`src/services/videoAssetEngine.cjs`)
- **Video Concepts:** Exactly 30 concepts per product × 10 products = **300 Video Concepts**.
- **Categorization per Product:**
  - 10 Short Reels (15–20 sec)
  - 5 Educational Videos (30–35 sec)
  - 5 FAQ Videos (30 sec)
  - 5 Problem / Solution Videos (40 sec)
  - 5 Lead-Generation Videos (20 sec)
- **Primary Formats:** `9:16` Vertical Reel and `1:1` Square.
- **Components Included:** Hook (0–3s), Problem (3–10s), Solution (10–25s), Avani Guidance (25–35s), CTA (35–45s), Voice-Over, On-Screen Text, B-Roll, and Camera Direction.

### C. Content Quality & Compliance Scorer (`src/services/contentQualityScorer.cjs`)
- **Automated 0–100 Scoring Algorithm** covering:
  1. Regulatory Compliance (0–20 pts)
  2. Brand Consistency (0–20 pts)
  3. CTA Quality (0–15 pts)
  4. Readability & Formatting (0–15 pts)
  5. Product Relevance (0–10 pts)
  6. Audience Relevance (0–10 pts)
  7. Language & Natural Fluency (0–5 pts)
  8. Uniqueness & Duplicate Detection (0–5 pts)
- **Quality Thresholds:**
  - `90–100`: **READY** (Permitted to enter Meta / AiSensy publishing workflow)
  - `75–89`: **REVIEW** (Flagged for manual review)
  - `< 75`: **REWRITE** (Hard-blocked from publishing)

### D. 11-State Publishing State Machine (`src/services/publishingStateMachine.cjs`)
- Enforces strict sequential transitions without state skipping:
  `DRAFT` ➔ `VALIDATED` ➔ `READY_FOR_SUBMISSION` ➔ `SUBMITTED_TO_META` ➔ `META_PENDING` ➔ `META_APPROVED` ➔ `META_REJECTED` ➔ `READY_FOR_AISENSY` ➔ `PUBLISHED_TO_AISENSY` ➔ `FAILED` ➔ `ARCHIVED`.
- Meta Approval Dependency: Content cannot enter `READY_FOR_AISENSY` or `PUBLISHED_TO_AISENSY` unless confirmed as `META_APPROVED`.

### E. Bulk Operations & Mandatory Human Confirmation Guard
- Bulk Meta submission requires explicit confirmation via `{ confirmed: true }`.
- Templates with quality score below 75 are auto-rejected prior to API submission.

### F. 30-Day Product Content Calendar (`src/services/contentCalendarEngine.cjs`)
- Multi-channel orchestration across WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp Status.
- Supported exports: Direct JSON and Excel-compatible CSV via `/api/templates/calendar?format=csv`.
