## Guide: How to Broadcast Your Images & Videos via WhatsApp

You asked how to send the files located in your local folders:
- `C:\Users\ALPHA-1\Downloads\21MAY2026\SACHIN SHINDE DOCUMENTS\AVANI LOAN SERVICES\IMAGE`
- `C:\Users\ALPHA-1\Downloads\21MAY2026\SACHIN SHINDE DOCUMENTS\AVANI LOAN SERVICES\SOCIAL MEDIA VIDEOS`

### 1. The Meta Rule
Meta does NOT allow you to just upload a video and broadcast it randomly. You must create a **Media Message Template** in your Meta Business account and get it approved.

### 2. How to get them approved
1. Go to your [WhatsApp Manager (Meta Business Suite)](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/message-templates/).
2. Click **Create Template**.
3. Choose **Marketing** -> **Custom**.
4. In the **Header** section, choose **Media**, and select **Image** or **Video**.
5. **Upload one of your local files** (e.g., from your `IMAGE` folder) as a "Sample".
6. Write your caption text and add buttons.
7. Submit for Review (it usually takes 1-5 minutes).

### 3. How to Broadcast them in AVANI CRM
Once Meta approves the template, you must host your image/video online to send it.

1. **Upload your image/video** to a reliable public host (e.g., Amazon S3, Google Drive with a direct link, or Imgur for images).
2. Go to the **AVANI AI CRM -> Broadcasts Page**.
3. Select the template name exactly as you named it in Meta (e.g., `diwali_offer_video`).
4. Select the format (Image/Video).
5. Paste the **Public URL** of your uploaded file into the "Media File URL" box.
6. Click **Send Bulk Broadcast**. 

The CRM will pass the URL to Meta, and Meta will securely deliver the media and caption to your customers!
