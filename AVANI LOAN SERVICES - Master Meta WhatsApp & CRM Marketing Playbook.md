## STRATEGIC CRM PLAYBOOKS

### 1. Complete Lead Nurturing Journey (Day 0 to Day 30)
- **Day 0:** Instant AI voice call -> Automatic Webhook to CRM -> Send `New Lead Welcome` WhatsApp template.
- **Day 1:** Send `Eligibility Check` or `Document Request` if documents not yet received.
- **Day 3:** Follow-up Call (Manual/AI) + WhatsApp `crm_doc_pending_reminder`.
- **Day 7:** Send Educational Content ("Top 5 things to check before taking a loan").
- **Day 14:** Soft check-in via WhatsApp ("Still looking for funding? Let's connect.")
- **Day 30:** Move to "Cold Lead" pipeline; trigger cross-sell or CIBIL improvement campaign.

### 2. WhatsApp Automation Workflow (Make.com / Pabbly)
1. **Trigger:** New Vapi Webhook Lead (or Webform).
2. **Action 1:** Filter -> If `interested == true`, proceed.
3. **Action 2:** Check `loanType` variable.
4. **Action 3:** Router branches to specific Meta WhatsApp API POST requests based on `loanType`.
5. **Action 4:** Insert Lead into HubSpot CRM.

### 3. CRM Pipeline Stages
1. **New AI Lead:** Automatically captured.
2. **Contacted:** Initial conversation happened.
3. **Eligibility Review:** Analyzing financials.
4. **Documents Requested:** Waiting for customer uploads.
5. **Application Submitted:** Sent to Bank/NBFC.
6. **Query Resolution:** Bank requested more info.
7. **Sanctioned:** Approval received.
8. **Disbursed:** Funds transferred (Closed Won).
9. **Rejected / Low CIBIL:** Needs credit repair.
10. **Not Interested:** Closed Lost.

### 4. Meta Approved CTA Suggestions
- **Buttons:** "Talk to Advisor", "Upload Securely", "Check Eligibility", "Schedule Call"
- **Avoid:** "Get Instant Cash", "100% Approval", "Click Here for Money"

### 5. High-Converting Follow-up Sequence
- **Focus on Value:** Remind them *why* they applied (home, education, business growth).
- **Urgency without spam:** "Current interest rate offers are valid till end of the month."
- **Authority:** "Sachin Shinde brings 10+ years of advisory experience to ensure you get the best terms."

### 6. Lead Recovery Campaign
Target leads stuck in "Not Interested" or "Unresponsive" for 60+ days.
*Template:* "Hello {{1}}, the financial market has shifted recently, introducing new loan schemes with better terms. Would you like a free review of your current funding needs?"

### 7. Referral Campaign
Target "Disbursed" clients after 30 days.
*Template:* "Hello {{1}}, know someone looking for financial advisory? Refer them to AVANI LOAN SERVICES and help them secure their goals seamlessly!"

### 8. Cross-Selling Campaign
Target Personal Loan clients after 6 months for Top-ups or Credit Cards. Target Business Loan clients for Mortgages or Equipment Finance.

### 9. Monthly WhatsApp Marketing Calendar
- **Week 1:** Educational Broadcast (e.g., "How to maintain a 750+ CIBIL").
- **Week 2:** Client Success Story (e.g., "How a local clinic expanded using our Doctor Loan").
- **Week 3:** Service Highlight (e.g., "Understanding Education Loans for Global Studies").
- **Week 4:** Direct Offer/Check-in to engaged leads.
