I can send you our quick eligibility form via WhatsApp and you can fill it anytime. Then a loan officer will call you at your convenience."

"Perfect! I'm sending you our personal loan offer on WhatsApp. Please check within an hour and reply 'Ready' if you want to proceed. Our loan officer will call you within 30 minutes to finalize the documents. Your reference ID is [CALL_ID]. Have a great day!"
