### 2. THE FIVE KNOWN PROBLEMS & THEIR FORENSIC RESOLUTIONS

#### Problem A: Score Reconciliation
- **The Issue:** The Phase 3 report previously displayed conflicting statewide averages: Section 6 cited 97.6/100, while Section 7's city table averaged 95.3/100.
- **Forensic Diagnosis:** The Section 6 text contained an erroneous reference to Thane's individual average (97.62/100), whereas the true sum of all 80 individual V3 scores was 7628.0.
- **The Resolution:** 
  $$\text{True Statewide Mean} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{80} \text{Score}_i}{80} = \frac{7628.0}{80} = 95.35 / 100$$
  The arithmetic average of the 10 city averages is also exactly $\frac{953.5}{10} = 95.35$.
  All individual scores are verified between 71.0 and 100.0. Grade distribution is strictly reconciled: **77 Grade A (Score $\ge 80$)** and **3 Grade B (Score 60–79)**.

#### Problem B: Decision-Maker Reconciliation
- **The Issue:** The Phase 3 report identified 56 verified current decision-makers and 20 public company contacts, yet the CRM export previously described all 70 qualified contacts as "verified decision-makers".
- **Forensic Diagnosis:** Switchboard and company-routed contacts were improperly aggregated under the "verified decision-maker" label in the CRM summary.
- **The Resolution:** Enforced 4 separate, mutually exclusive categories:
  1. `VERIFIED DECISION-MAKER`: 52 contacts (High-level executive with direct dedicated mobile/WA).
  2. `NAMED EXECUTIVE — CONTACT ROUTED THROUGH COMPANY`: 24 contacts (17 in CRM pipeline, 7 on research hold).
  3. `COMPANY SALES / SWITCHBOARD CONTACT`: 1 contact (Bedekar Lifespaces sales desk).
  4. `DECISION-MAKER NOT VERIFIED`: 3 contacts (All on research hold).
  Total: $52 + 24 + 1 + 3 = 80$ builders. Never is a general company line described as a direct executive contact.

#### Problem C: Outreach Approval Reconciliation
- **The Issue:** Unclear boundary between immediate outreach eligibility and secondary verification.
- **The Resolution:** Formulated strict qualifying conditions: A builder is `APPROVED FOR IMMEDIATE OUTREACH` only if company existence, residential projects, MahaRERA promoter match, decision-maker identity, and direct contact channel are all 100% verified with zero unresolved anomalies.
  - **Immediate Outreach:** 52 Builders (65.0%)
  - **Contact Confirmation Required:** 18 Builders (22.5%)
  - **Research Hold:** 10 Builders (12.5%)

#### Problem D: MahaRERA Revalidation
- **The Issue:** Potential risk of conflating holding brands with separate legal promoter entities.
- **The Resolution:** Every registration was re-inspected against official portal filings. Group entities and joint ventures were audited:
  - 70 records confirmed as exact `VERIFIED MATCH`.
  - 6 records confirmed as `VALID NUMBER — WRONG PROMOTER` (e.g. Rosa citing Raunak's RERA, Ghatge/Vasant citing Niwara's RERA, Nirmal/Vrushali citing cluster promoter, Shreeji citing Samraat's RERA).
  - 4 records confirmed as `PROJECT STATUS NOT CONFIRMED` (Lakhani, Bhagwati, Mahalaxmi, Kasliwal).
  All 10 discrepant cases are placed on strict research hold.

#### Problem E: Source Evidence Validation
- **The Issue:** Ensuring zero synthetic or hallucinated URLs.
- **The Resolution:** Captured exact source URLs, source types, claim dates, and limitations for all 80 builders in [`Phase3_1_Evidence_Register.xlsx`](file:///c:/Users/ALPHA-1/Downloads/21MAY2026/SACHIN%20SHINDE%20DOCUMENTS/DEVELOPEMENT%20TOOLS/1-AVANI%20LOAN%20SERVICE%20FY%2026-27/Phase3_1_Evidence_Register.xlsx).
