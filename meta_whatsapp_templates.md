# AVANI LOAN SERVICES - Meta WhatsApp Templates

This document contains highly optimized, Meta-compliant broadcast templates for your 10 distinct loan funnels and your business introduction.

Meta is strict about spam, so these templates are designed as **Utility/Marketing** messages that are professional, clear, and highly likely to be approved instantly.

---

## 📝 General Instructions for Meta Manager
1. Go to **WhatsApp Manager > Message Templates > Create Template**.
2. Select **Marketing** as the category.
3. Name your template using lowercase letters and underscores (e.g., `avani_business_intro`).
4. Under **Header**, choose **Media** and select Image, Video, or Document based on the instructions below.
5. Copy and paste the **Body** text exactly as written below.
6. Under **Buttons**, choose **Quick Reply** or **Call to Action** as suggested below.

## 💡 Pro-Tip for Sending Broadcasts:
When you upload your contact list (CSV) into your CRM to send these, make sure the CSV has a column for `Name`. The `{{1}}` in the templates above will automatically pull the customer's Name, making the message highly personalized! If you don't have their name, you can replace `{{1}}` with "there" (e.g. *Hello there!*) before submitting to Meta.
