# AVANI LOAN SERVICES - META WHATSAPP & CRM TEMPLATES

This document contains highly professional, conversion-focused, and Meta-compliant WhatsApp Business Templates and CRM Automation Workflows for AVANI LOAN SERVICES. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Meta Compliance Note:** All templates are drafted to comply with Meta's strict Financial Services policies. They avoid guarantees, misleading promises, and spammy language. Before submitting to the Meta WhatsApp Manager, ensure you replace the `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` variables with sample values.

## A. PERSONAL LOAN

**1. New Lead Welcome & Eligibility Check**
* **Template Name:** `pl_new_lead_welcome`
* **Category:** Marketing
* **Purpose:** Acknowledge personal loan inquiry and initiate qualification.
* **Template Body:** 
  Hello {{1}},
  Thank you for choosing AVANI LOAN SERVICES. We received your Personal Loan inquiry. 
  To help us check your eligibility quickly and provide the best options, please reply with 'YES' to answer 4 quick questions.
* **Variables:** `{{1}}` = Customer Name
* **Buttons:** `Quick Reply: YES, Continue` | `Quick Reply: Talk to Advisor`
* **Meta Approval Probability:** High
* **Automation Trigger:** Lead created in CRM via Meta Lead Form.
* **Recommended CRM Workflow:** Move to `CONTACTED`. Wait for 'YES' to trigger the AI Agent eligibility sequence.

**2. Document Request**
* **Template Name:** `pl_document_request`
* **Category:** Utility
* **Purpose:** Request KYC and income documents.
* **Template Body:** 
  Dear {{1}},
  Great news! Based on your profile, you are eligible to proceed with your Personal Loan application. 
  Please share clear photos/PDFs of the following:
  1. PAN & Aadhaar
  2. Last 3 months Salary Slips
  3. Last 6 months Bank Statements
  Upload securely here: {{2}}
* **Variables:** `{{1}}` = Customer Name, `{{2}}` = Document Upload Link
* **Buttons:** `URL: Upload Documents`
* **Meta Approval Probability:** High
* **Automation Trigger:** AI Agent tags lead as `PL-HOT` or `PL-WARM`.

## STRATEGIES & CAMPAIGNS

### 1. Complete Lead Nurturing Journey (Day 0 to Day 30)
* **Day 0:** Instant Welcome Message & AI Qualification (`NEW_LEAD` -> `QUALIFIED`)
* **Day 1:** Document Request Reminder (Utility Template)
* **Day 3:** Educational message about AVANI's processing speed (Marketing Template)
* **Day 7:** "Still looking for a loan?" Re-engagement prompt.
* **Day 15:** Cross-sell alternative products (e.g., if Personal Loan rejected, offer CIBIL consultation).
* **Day 30:** Final courtesy closure message. Move to `CLOSED (Lost)` or place in long-term nurture sequence.

### 2. CRM Pipeline Stages
1. **NEW LEAD** - Captured via Meta Lead Ads
2. **CONTACTED** - Welcome message sent
3. **QUALIFIED** - AI Agent successfully asked all questions
4. **DOCUMENTS PENDING** - Waiting on customer KYC/Financials
5. **DOCUMENTS RECEIVED** - Customer uploaded all PDFs/Images
6. **ELIGIBILITY CHECK** - Internal review
7. **BANK DISCUSSION** - File logged in to partner banks
8. **APPLICATION SUBMITTED** - Final processing
9. **SANCTION RECEIVED** - Approved!
10. **DISBURSED** - Funds transferred
11. **CLOSED** - Won or Lost

### 3. Monthly WhatsApp Marketing Calendar
* **Week 1:** Broadcast `cibil_consultation` to cold leads to re-engage them.
* **Week 2:** Broadcast `prof_special_scheme` specifically to tagged Doctors and CAs.
* **Week 3:** Broadcast `hl_eligibility_check` emphasizing low interest rates for festive seasons.
* **Week 4:** Broadcast `crm_review_request` to all clients who had their loans disbursed in the last 30 days.

### 4. Meta Approved CTA Suggestions
* `Apply Now` (URL to website)
* `Upload Documents` (URL to secure portal)
* `Check Eligibility` (Quick Reply)
* `Talk to Sachin` (Quick Reply)
* `View Sanction Letter` (URL)

> [!TIP]
> **Pro-Tip for Image/Video Broadcasts:** When creating these templates in the Meta WhatsApp Manager, select **Media** as the header type instead of Text. Upload a branded image (e.g., AVANI LOAN SERVICES logo with "Fast Personal Loans"). Once Meta approves it, you can blast it via the CRM broadcasts page!
