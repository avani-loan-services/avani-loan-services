## 1. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC STRATEGIES

### 1.1 Facebook & Instagram (Meta)
* **Goal:** High volume lead generation and brand awareness.
* **Targeting:** Geo-targeted to Maharashtra (specifically Latur and surrounding regions), age 25-55. 
* **Content:** Short-form video (Reels) explaining loan benefits, Carousel posts showing EMI comparisons, and Success Story graphics.
* **Ads Strategy:** Run "Click-to-WhatsApp" Ads. When a user clicks your ad, it opens a chat with your WhatsApp Business number. Your CRM then automatically replies and captures the lead.

### 1.2 LinkedIn
* **Goal:** B2B networking, high-ticket Business Loans, and Doctor Loans.
* **Targeting:** Job titles (Doctors, CAs, Architects, Engineers, Business Owners, Founders).
* **Content:** Professional articles on business expansion, market trends in real estate, and financial planning tips. Text-heavy posts with single professional images.
* **Ads Strategy:** LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms targeted specifically at Medical Professionals and MSME Owners.

### 1.3 Pinterest
* **Goal:** Long-term organic traffic and SEO.
* **Targeting:** Users searching for "Home Buying Tips," "Study Abroad Guide," "Financial Planning."
* **Content:** Highly visual infographics (e.g., "5 Steps to Improve Your CIBIL Score", "Checklist for Buying Your First Home"). 
* **Ads Strategy:** Promoted Pins directing traffic directly to your website's application forms.

### 1.4 Google My Business (GMB) & Other Sites
* **Goal:** Local SEO and trust building.
* **Content:** Weekly updates on loan interest rate drops, photos of your office, and posting 5-star customer reviews. Ensure your address (Rajiv Gandhi Chauk) and website are highly visible.

## 2. THE CONVERSION FUNNEL (How to turn Likes into Revenue)

To generate revenue, do not just ask for "likes." Every post must lead to a conversion action.

1. **The Hook:** An eye-catching graphic or video addressing a pain point (e.g., "Business expanding but short on capital?").
2. **The Lead Magnet:** Offer a free consultation or a free CIBIL score check.
3. **The CTA (Call to Action):** "Click the link in bio to chat with us on WhatsApp" or "DM us 'LOAN' to apply."
4. **The Capture:** The user clicks the link, lands on your CRM WhatsApp bot, and becomes a captured lead in your database.

## 4. SCHEDULING & POSTING PROCEDURES

To maintain a 30-day calendar without losing your mind, you must use **Social Media Scheduling Tools**. Do not post manually every day.

### Tool 1: Meta Business Suite (Free - Best for Facebook & Instagram)
1. **Connect Accounts:** Ensure your Instagram Professional account is linked to your Facebook Business Page.
2. **Access:** Go to `business.facebook.com`.
3. **Planner:** Click on the "Planner" tab on the left sidebar.
4. **Create:** Click "Create Post".
5. **Upload:** Paste your caption from the 30-Day calendar above, and upload your image/video.
6. **Schedule:** Instead of clicking "Publish", click the arrow next to it and select "Schedule". Pick the date and time (e.g., Day 1 at 10:00 AM).
7. *Batching:* Spend 2 hours every Sunday scheduling the entire week's content.

### Tool 2: Buffer or Hootsuite (Freemium - Best for LinkedIn & Pinterest)
1. **Signup:** Create a free account on `buffer.com`.
2. **Connect:** Link your LinkedIn Profile/Page and Pinterest account.
3. **Queue:** Click "Create Post", select both LinkedIn and Pinterest.
4. **Customize:** Tailor the caption slightly (make LinkedIn more professional, add Pinterest-specific visual sizes).
5. **Add to Queue:** Select the date and time and hit "Schedule".

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip for Automation:** Always include your `wa.me/919175635165` link in your posts. When people click it, your CRM auto-mode will instantly capture them as a lead without you having to manually type their details!
