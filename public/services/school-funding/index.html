School Funding & Infrastructure Finance

Avani Loan Services provides institutional credit for private schools and educational trusts across Maharashtra. Whether you need to build new classrooms, install smart boards, purchase school buses, or modernize science labs, we match your institution with the right funding structure.

Key Uses

  • Classroom wing construction and auditorium development
  • Smart classroom, AV equipment and digital lab setup
  • School bus fleet purchase and renewal
  • Playground and sports infrastructure development

Eligibility

  • Registered educational trust or society
  • Government or board recognition (CBSE / ICSE / State Board)
  • 3 years of audited financial statements

Documents

  • Trust / Society Registration & Trust Deed
  • Board recognition and affiliation letters
  • 3 years audited financials and bank statements
  • Campus land title / lease deed and building plans
