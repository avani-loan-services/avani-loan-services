## 📋 How to Submit These to Meta (Copy-Paste Instructions):

1. Go to your **WhatsApp Manager** -> **Message Templates**.
2. Click **Create Template**.
3. Category: **Marketing**, Type: **Custom**, Language: **English (US) or English (UK)**.
4. **Header**: Choose "Text" and paste the Header text.
5. **Body**: Paste the Body text. Where you see `{{1}}`, Meta will recognize it as a variable (you can map this to the customer's name in your CRM).
6. **Footer**: Paste "Reply STOP to unsubscribe".
7. **Buttons**: Add Quick Reply buttons or URL/Phone number buttons exactly as shown.
8. Click **Submit**. Meta usually approves these within 1 to 5 minutes!
