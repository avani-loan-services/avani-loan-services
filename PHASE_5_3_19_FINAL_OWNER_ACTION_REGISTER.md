### 2. HOW TO VIEW AND TEST ON MOBILE

1. Open your mobile browser (Safari, Chrome) and navigate to `https://www.avanifinserv.com/admin`.
2. Sign in with your administrator username and password.
3. Notice that the top navigation pills hide automatically to keep the header clean.
4. Tap the **Hamburger Menu (≡)** in the top right header to reveal the full off-canvas navigation drawer:
   - **Main:** Overview, Leads CRM
   - **Databases:** Builder Database (80), School Funding (24), College & Univ (56)
   - **Outreach:** Templates, Campaigns, Analytics
   - **Operations:** Tasks, Enquiries, Reports & Exports
   - **System:** Automation, Security Settings, Audit Logs
5. Tap any section to instantly jump to that database or tool.
