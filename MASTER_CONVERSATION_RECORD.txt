4. AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS (MAKE.COM)
===================================

Dashboard: https://eu1.make.com/organization/7622610/dashboard

Primary Workflow: VAPI → HubSpot → Google Sheets → WhatsApp

Flow Steps:
1. Trigger: Receive webhook from VAPI call completion
2. Parse: Extract structured data from analysis field
3. Action 1: Create/Update contact in HubSpot
   - Map all fields from VAPI analysis
   - Set pipeline stage based on qualification_status
   - Assign to loan officer based on loan_product
4. Action 2: Append row to Google Sheets
   - Columns: timestamp, name, phone, loan_product, qualification_status, next_action
5. Action 3: Send WhatsApp message (if next_action = "send_whatsapp")
   - Message: Loan offer + documents + next steps
6. Action 4: Create task/reminder (if next_action = "schedule_followup")
7. Action 5: Send SMS reminder (if appointment_scheduled)

Error Handling:
- If HubSpot sync fails: Retry 3 times with 5-min intervals
- If Google Sheets fails: Alert via email
- If WhatsApp fails: Log and retry next day

PERSONAL LOAN SCRIPT
--------------------
Opening:
"Hello, this is Sachin from Avani Loan Services, Latur. Am I speaking with [Name]? 
I'm calling to tell you that you might be eligible for an instant personal loan up to ₹10 lakh. 
Do you have a quick 2 minutes to check your eligibility?"

Key Questions:
1. "Are you currently employed or self-employed?"
2. "What is your approximate monthly income or salary?"
3. "Do you have any existing loans or EMIs? If yes, how much?"
4. "How much loan amount are you looking for?"
5. "What would you need the loan for? (Home renovation, marriage, education, etc.)"

Qualifying Statement:
"Based on your income of ₹[amount] and current EMI situation, you look like a strong candidate 
for our personal loan. Our approval time is typically 24 hours. 
Can I share our loan documents and requirements via WhatsApp so you can review them?"

"Thank you for the information. Our personal loan requires a minimum monthly income of ₹15,000. 
However, you might qualify for our flexible unsecured loan. Can I schedule a call from our loan officer tomorrow?"

"I'm not interested." 
→ "I understand. The reason I'm calling is because there's a special offer this week.

→ "That's completely fine. I can send you our quick eligibility form via WhatsApp 
and you can fill it anytime. Then a loan officer will call you at your convenience."

Closing:
"Perfect! I'm sending you our personal loan offer on WhatsApp. 
Please check within an hour and reply 'Ready' if you want to proceed. 
Our loan officer will call you within 30 minutes to finalize the documents. 
Your reference ID is [CALL_ID]. Have a great day!"
