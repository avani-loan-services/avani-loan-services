# AVANI LOAN SERVICES — PHASE 5.3.16 ADMIN USER GUIDE
## EXECUTIVE CRM & THREE-TIER INSTITUTIONAL DATABASE USER MANUAL

**Document Reference:** `ALS-GUIDE-P5316-20261106`  
**Target Audience:** Avani Loan Services Executive Management, Alliance Directors, Lending Officers, and Research Analysts  
**Access Portal:** `https://www.avanifinserv.com/admin/login` (or `/admin/login` on any live domain)

---

### 1. LOGGING IN TO THE SECURE PORTAL

1. Navigate to the Avani Loan Services home page (`https://www.avanifinserv.com/`).
2. Click the **Admin Access** button with the lock icon in the top navigation bar (or in the mobile navigation drawer).
3. On the login screen, enter your assigned administrative credentials:
   - **Username / Email:** `admin` (or your personal corporate email).
   - **Password:** Enter your secure password.
4. Click **Sign In to Management Portal**.
   - *Note:* If this is your first time logging in or your account was reset, you will see a prompt to enter a new permanent password of at least 8 characters.

---

---

### 3. USING THE BUILDER DATABASE (80 RECORDS)

1. **Switching Sub-views:**
   - Click any subview pill tab above the data table:
     - **All Builders (80):** Full developer inventory across Maharashtra.
     - **Residential Developers (80):** High-rise & township developers for home loan counters.
     - **Builder Contacts (80):** Corporate alliance desks and marketing managers.
     - **Decision Makers (79):** Managing Directors, Promoters, and Chief Financial Officers.
     - **Partnership Pipeline (80):** Active deal pipeline stages.
     - **Follow-up Tasks (0):** Pending tasks.
2. **Filtering by City:**
   - Use the City dropdown filter to select from: *Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, Thane, Aurangabad, Kolhapur, Solapur, Amravati, Nanded*.
3. **Searching:**
   - Type in the search box to filter instantly by Developer Name, City, or RERA Number.
4. **Detail Drawer Inspection:**
   - Click the **Inspect** button or any table row. A side drawer slides in displaying the developer's full dossier, project inventory, units, RERA numbers, and decision maker contacts.
5. **Direct WhatsApp Chat:**
   - In the detail drawer, click **WhatsApp Alliance Chat** to immediately open a chat with the developer's contact executive.
6. **Exporting to Excel:**
   - Click the green **Export Excel (XLSX)** button to download `Builder_Database_Master.xlsx`.

---

### 4. USING THE SCHOOL FUNDING DATABASE (24 RECORDS)

1. **Research-Based Estimates Protocol:**
   - Notice the prominent yellow warning banner at the top of the School Database explaining the difference between confirmed regulatory identities (trust names, CBSE/ICSE board affiliations, campus addresses) and research-based analytical estimates (student capacity, approx enrolment, funding need).
2. **Sub-views Available:**
   - **All Schools (24):** Complete school directory.
   - **School Groups & Chains (23):** Multi-campus school foundations.
   - **Education Trusts (24):** Registered public charitable trusts.
   - **Priority Prospects A+ (24):** High-potential campuses for expansion loans.
   - **Funding Pipeline (24):** Active loan proposals.
3. **Exporting to Excel:**
   - Click **Export Schools (XLSX)** to download `School_Funding_Database_Master.xlsx`.

---

### 5. USING THE COLLEGE & UNIVERSITY DATABASE (56 RECORDS)

1. **Sub-views Available:**
   - **All Campuses (56):** Master list of all higher education institutions.
   - **Engineering Colleges (50):** Technical and technological universities.
   - **Medical Colleges & Hospitals (22):** Medical colleges, teaching hospitals, and dental institutes.
   - **Autonomous Institutes (19):** Colleges with UGC academic autonomy.
   - **Deemed Universities (14):** Deemed-to-be and private universities.
   - **Education Trusts (56):** Governing societies.
   - **Priority Prospects A+ (28):** Tier-1 balance sheet prospects.
   - **Collegiate Pipeline (56):** Deal stage tracker.
2. **Exporting to Excel:**
   - Click **Export Colleges (XLSX)** to download `College_University_Funding_Master.xlsx`.

---

### 6. EMERGENCY STOP & AUTOMATION CONTROLS

- To instantly halt all outbound campaigns, navigate to the **Automation & System** tab.
- Click **Activate Emergency STOP**. This immediately disables all background dispatches while keeping inbound public enquiry forms and database viewing 100% operational.
- Only a **Super Administrator** can resume automated outbound services.
